## What is the suns structure
- Gravitational equilibrium is where the energy by fusion maintains the pressure that balances the inward crush of gravity. AKA hydrostatic equilibrium is pressure on the outside equaling the pressure on the inside
- Contraction of the sun stopped when fusion began
- The sun has a core where the energy is produced. Where hydrogen is fused into helium
- The energy from the core is carried out by the radiation zone
- This energy is carried by the convection layer to the surface photosphere
- The corona is where the solar wind starts
- The deeper you go in the sun, the higher pressure and thus the higher temperature
- Nuclear fusion
  - At high speeds, temperatures, and pressures hydrogen nuclei come close enough for the strong force to bind the ionized (made of protons) hydrogen together
