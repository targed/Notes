# The first three minutes of the universe
- The early universe was extremely hot and extremely dense
- More heat means more energy and thus the universe was extremely energetic in the beginning
- 1 second after the universe formed it was around 1 million kelvin degrees
- We can only go back to the plank time because we don’t know what happened before it
## Quantum field theory
- The standard model equation

- There are also elementary particles
- ![2024-04-26-15-14-03.jpeg](../assets/2024-04-26-15-14-03.jpeg)
- There are quarks, leptons, fermions, gauge bosons, etc
- Most matter in the universe is just made of up and down quarks and electrons
- We know about these particles because we have observed them in our particle accelerators
- There are other types of elementary particles called bosons which are responsible for the interactions between particles.
- The bosons are responsible for the forces and interactions between other particles
- The photon is responsible for the electromagnetic interaction
- We have not found the graviton yet. It would be the particle responsible for gravity
- The Higgs particle is responsible for giving mass to all other particles

## Particle creation and annihilation
- The more mater interactions you have, the more chances you have for particles to interact
- As the energy increases, you blur the line between particles to create a “particle soup”
- ![2024-04-26-15-33-38.jpeg](../assets/2024-04-26-15-33-38.jpeg)
- The early universe is a mix of soup and radiation in equilibrium
## The first 3 minutes
- The plank era
  - There was no theory of quantum gravity
- GUT era
  - Strong, weak, and EM forces are unified
- Electroweak era
  - The strong force is separated from the electroweak force
  - At the end, the weak force separates from the electromagnetic force
- Particle era
  - Amount of mater and antimatter is nearly equal
  - There was just a little more mater than antimatter
- Era of nucleosyntheis
  - It happens when mater annihilates the antimatter
  - Nuclei begin to fuse
- Era of nuclei
  - Helium nuclei form
  - Background radiation is released
- Era of galaxies
  - Galaxies start forming after around 300 million years
## Lasting mysteries
- We still don’t know
  - Where do cosmological large scale structures come from
    - Inflation can make the cosmological structures by attaching tiny quantum ripples into enormous sizes
    - These ripples in density then become the seeds for all the structures in the universe
  - Why is the overall distribution of matter so uniform
    - Things will always tend to be the same over time
  - Why is the universe so big and flat
