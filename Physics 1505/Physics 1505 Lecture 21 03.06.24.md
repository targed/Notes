## Solar activity
- The sunspot cycle has something to do with the winding and twisting of the sun’s magnetic field
- The magnetic field is created by the charged plasma of the sun so if you move the plasma, you move the magnetic field
- Since the sun is rotates at different speeds at different latitudes, the magnetic field lines twist into each other releasing a lot of energy
- Every 11 years the suns magnetic field lines get so entangled that they reset to not being tangled
- Despite the 11 year sun cycle, the amount of sunlight that earth has received has remained constant for a long time
# Stars
## How do we measure stellar properties
- Luminosity
  - Luminosity is not brightness
  - Luminosity is the amount of energy that is released by the star per unit of time: AKA watts
  - The amount of luminosity passing through each sphere is the same
  - ![2024-03-06-15-23-29.jpeg](../assets/2024-03-06-15-23-29.jpeg)
  - ![2024-03-06-15-27-18.jpeg](../assets/2024-03-06-15-27-18.jpeg)
  - Based on the magnitude scale, the greater the luminosity the less the magnitude
  - Magnitude is a logarithmic scale. A star with a negative magnitude measurement is more luminous than a star with a positive magnitude measurement
- Apparent brightness
  - Amount of starlight that reaches earth (energy per second per square meter)
  - Divide luminosity by area to get brightness
  - ![2024-03-06-15-25-26.jpeg](../assets/2024-03-06-15-25-26.jpeg)
- Wines law
  - ![2024-03-06-15-36-10.jpeg](../assets/2024-03-06-15-36-10.jpeg)
  - Stars that look red are cooler than stars that look blue
  - The hottest stars are around 50000K while the coolest stars are around 3000k
- Chemical composition
  - You take a spectrum analysis of the stars light to tell what chemicals it is made of
  - ![2024-03-06-15-39-38.jpeg](../assets/2024-03-06-15-39-38.jpeg)
  - Lines in a stars spectrum correspond to a spectral type that reveals its temperature
  - (Hottest) O B A F G K M (Coolest)
- Stellar masses
  - ![2024-03-06-15-43-54.jpeg](../assets/2024-03-06-15-43-54.jpeg)
  - It is difficult to measure the mass of a star
  - A good measurement of a star can only happen if the star is a binary pair
  - ![2024-03-06-15-45-13.jpeg](../assets/2024-03-06-15-45-13.jpeg)
  - The orbit of a binary star system depends on the strength of the gravity
  - Stars that are more massive are bluer and have a higher luminosity
  - Stars that are smaller are more red and have a lower luminosity
