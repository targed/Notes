# Milky Way and other galaxies
## What does our galaxy look like
- The primary features of the Milky Way consist of the disk, bulge, halo, and globular clusters
- The Milky Way is a flat structure with a bulge in the center that is 100,000 light years across
- The sun is about 2/3rds from the center of the Milky Way -27,000 light years
- Outside of the Milky Way there are clusters of stars called globular clusters
- The Milky Way is a spiral galaxy rotating around a central black hole
- The milky ways nucleus has a very active region surrounding the black hole that is about 600 light years
- When you look through the Milky Way, you are seeing the projections of the other arms of the galaxy
## Motion of stars
- The Milky Way has the spiral structure because it is not a rigid object and thus the stars orbit
- The stars and interstellar gas pack into arms because of the friction of orbiting around
- The sun completes and orbit around the galaxy around every 50 million years

## Wavelengths
- The Milky Way emits many different wavelengths
- The galaxy is opaque to visible light by somewhat transparent to radio and other wavelengths
- Radio waves from carbon monoxide show the levitations of molecular clouds
- Most of the hydrogen and carbon monoxide is concentrated in the galactic plane
- Long wavelength infrared immersions shows where young stars are heating dust grains
- The galactic dust tends to diffuse around the galaxy
- Infrared light reveals stars whose viewable light is blocked by gas clouds
- X-Rays are observed from hot gas above and below the milky ways disck
- X-Rays and gamma ways are very energetic so they can only be created by events that make a large amounts of energy like black hole mergers, neutron stars, etc
- Gamma rays show where cosmic rays from supernovae collide with atomic nuclei in gas clouds
- Where you form stars in the galaxy is in the spiral arms

## The history
- Halo stars formed first then stopped
- Then the spiral arm formed later
- ![2024-04-03-15-42-39.jpeg](../assets/2024-04-03-15-42-39.jpeg)
- The Milky Way formed from the collapse of a intergalactic gas cloud that started fragmenting
- As it fragments, it forms the fist generation of stars
- As it this doing that, it begins to spin because of the conservation of angular momentum
- Then the arms form and the gas gets recycled
