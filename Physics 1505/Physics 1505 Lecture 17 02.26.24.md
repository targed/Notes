# Formation of the solar system and exoplanets
## Properties of the solar system
- The motion of the large bodies in the solar system orbit on the same plane
- There are two types of planets, terrestrial and Jovian
- There are many smaller bodies such as asteroids and comets
- There are unusual exceptions to these patterns such as Uranus rotating on its side, how big earths moon is, etc
- We need to create an accurate model of the solar system that includes and can explain the normal and abnormal characteristics of the system
# The nebular theory
- The nebular theory/model states that solar systems form from the gravitational collapse of clouds of interstellar gas
- This idea was proposed nearly 2 centuries ago by Kant and Lapance and there is now a lot of evidence that supports this theory
- With modern techniques, we can see the formation of other solar systems from nebulae
- Stars and solar systems from from proto-planetary discs
## Galactic recycling
- Elements that formed planets were made in stars and then recycled through interstellar space
- When stars explode, the send a shockwave with heavy elements that provides the seed material for planets
## Patterns of motion
- After the gas collapses, the gas formed a disc

- We have ground discs around other stars that confirm these theories
## the frost line
- If you are too close to the star, you will burn up
- If you are too far away from a star, you will freeze
- Within the frost line, gasses stay as gases
## Formation of plants
- The accretion discs of stuff within the frost line conglomerated together until they created the terrestrial planets
- The larger Jovian planets formed from the accretion of small ice crystals outside of the frost line
  - Larger planets accrete faster than smaller ones
- Leftover planetesimals bombarded objects in the late solar system
- One popular theory is that water is carried to planets through accretion of comets
- The giant impact theory is the idea that the moon was created by a late stage collision of two planetoids. The leftover material from the collision went on to form the moon
- However, there are many exceptions that we have seen in other solar systems
