# The universe
- The universe is old and is exp adding at an increasing rate
- The space between space is expanding
- Eventually space will expand past the speed of light
- We know this because of the Dopllar effect
  - It is the stretching and compression of waves ((light, sound, etc)
  - The further an object is, the more the objects light waves are stretched. They are red shifted
  - The closer an object is, the more the light waves are compressed. This is called blue shift
  - Because the universe is expanding, it is logical to assume that at a time in the past, space was a single point and started expanding. It is called the Big Bang theory
  - The cosmic microwave background radiation (CMBR) is the residual snapshot of the temperature fluctuations of the early universe when it transfers from opaque to visible
- Galaxies form from the collapse of intergalactic clouds
- The fluctuations in the CMBR are where galaxies, stars, and other stuff form
# How did the stars form
- Stars form from the collapse of interstellar gas
- After a certain critical mass is reached, it collapses and achieves nuclear fusion
- The left over gas forms the rest of the mater in the solar system, such as planets
- Stars die and explode into supernovae
# How do we measure the age of the solar system
- Radiometric dating can determine the age of the oldest rocks
  - If you know the half life and decay rate of the radioactive isotopes, you can determine its age
- Astronomical processes are very slow when compared to human timescales
- Using these processes, we have determined that the earth and the solar system formed around 4.5 billion years ago
- The there are some stars that formed around 10 billion years ago
- The fist stars and galaxies formed around 13 billion years ago
