# Jovian planets
- Are larger and have more mass than terrestrial planets
- Have a lower density than terrestrial planets
- There are 4 gas giants in our solar system
- The gas giants are composted of mostly hydrogen and helium and a few other heavy elements
- Hydrogen and helium are primordial elements
- Uranus and Neptune are a little more dense than Jupiter and Saturn because they are made of more complex compounds than hydrogen and helium
- Adding gas to Jovian planets compresses the underlying gas layers
- There is a maximum size and mass that Jovian planets can get before they become a star
- Greater compression is why Jupiter is not much larger than Saturn even though it is 3 times more massive
- Jovian planets are not quite spherical because they spin very fast and thus the centripetal forces bulge out the equator
- There are no solid surfaces in Jovian planets
- Have different layers that fire to compression are in different phases
  - Hydrogen acts like a metal at great pressures because its electrons move freely
- The core of Jupiter is about the size of earth
# Internal heat
- Saturn and Jupiter radiate twice as much energy as they receive from the sun
  - Energy most likely comes from differentiation but it is not proven
- The energy received is directly proportional to the inverse square law
# Atmospheres
- We can only see the top atmospheric layer of Jovian planets
- We use spectroscopic analysis of Jovian planets to tell the composition of their atmospheres
  - Ammonia sulfide reflects red/brown
  - Ammonia reflects white
- There are hydrogen compounds in Jupiter's atmosphere
  - Different cloud layers correspond to the different freezing points of the different hydrogen compounds
  - Jupiters Great Red Spot is a giant vortex that is bigger than earth
- Jupiter also has many atmospheric bands that go different directions
- Saturns atmosphere is very simpler to Jupiter but just scaled down because it is smaller
- Uranus and Neptune have a higher concentration of methane which is why the appear blue
# Magnetic fields
- Jovian planets have a massive magnetic field called a magnetosphere
  - It is created through the dynamo effect (the drag of spinning layers on each other fire to charge particles)
- The Jovian magnetic fields carry charge particles and energy
- Jupiter has metallic hydrogen which circulates and creates the magnetic feild
