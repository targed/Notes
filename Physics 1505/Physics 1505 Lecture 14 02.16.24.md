# Asteroids, comets, and dwarf planets
## Asteroids
- Asteroids are rocky leftovers of planet formation
- Most asteroids are in the asteroid belt and the kuiper belt
- Small asteroids are more common than larger asteroids
- There are around 150000 asteroids that are cataloged
  - There are probably over a million asteroids with a diameter greater than 1 km
- We usually find asteroids by taking long exposure shots of the sky and seeing what moves in that time
- Asteroids Are cratered and generally not round
- Some large asteroids have moons
- Measuring the orbit of an asteroids moon reels us an asteroids mass
- Mass and size tell us the density
## Comets
- Comets are the icy counterpoints to asteroids
- Nucleus of a comet is a collection of ice and rock
- Most comets remain perpetually frozen
- Only comets that enter the inner solar system grow tails because they start evaporating as they get closer to the sun
- A coma is the atmosphere that comes from a comets heated nucleus
- A plasma tail is gas escaping from coma which is pushed by the solar wind
- A dust tail is pushed by photons and extends away from the sun
- Comets come from the Kuiper Belt or the Oort Cloud
## Dwarf planets
- They are things that are not an asteroid, planet, or comet and are also round
- They may be similar to comets
- The moon of Pluto (Charon ) is almost as big as Pluto
- It is very cold (around 40K)
- It is still, somehow, geologically active
## Meteors and meteorites
- A meteorite is a rock from space that falls through earths atmosphere
- Meteor or bolide is the bright trail left be a meteorite
- There are many meteors that hit the earths atmosphere every day but most burn up
- There are two type of meteorites
  - Primitive meteorites are unchanged in their composition since they first formed 4.6 billion years ago
  - Processed meteorites have experienced erosion or volcanism
- Using chemical composition, you can tell where a meteorite comes from
- The asteroid belt exists because of the gravitational attraction of Jupiter
- Has prevented the formation of a planet in the asteroid belt
