# Phases of the moon
- Lunar phases are due to the moons orbit around the earth
- The sun only illuminates the part of the moon that is facing it, however earth can see the light and dark at the same time
- We only see one side of the moon at a time
  - The period of rotation for the moon is equal to the orbital period around earth
  - Thus, Earth will always see the same side of the moon
- There are craters on the south side of the moon that have never seen light
  - The moon has a small amount of water in the ice craters
# Eclipses
- Eclipses are caused by the shadow of the earth interacting with the sun and moon
- The shadow of the earth perfectly covers the moon crating a solar eclipse
  - Moons shadow falls also on earth
  - Solar eclipses can only occur at a new moon
  - Soccer eclipses can be partial, total, or annular
  - Annular eclipses occur when the moon is too far away to cover the sun
- When the earth is in the path between the moon and the sun it creates a lunar eclipse
  - Earths shadow falls on the moon
  - Lunar eclipse can only occur at full moon
  - Lunar eclipses can be penumbral, partial, or total
  - The atmosphere of earth refracted light onto the moon during the eclipse
  - Solar eclipses cannot be penumbral
