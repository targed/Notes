# The Copernican revolution
- Copernicus proposed the sun centric model
- He used the model to lay out where objects were in the solar system
- However, the model was not very accurate because it still used perfect circles for orbits
# Tycho Brahe
- He compiled the most accurate measurements of planetary positions
- Still thought that earth must be the center of the solar system but realized that other planets orbited around the sun
- He hired Kepler to dive deeper into planetary motion
- By trying to disprove Copernicus, he made extremely accurate measurements
# Johannes Kepler
- He discovered that the plane nets did not orbit in circles, instead they were ellipses
- He came up with the 3 laws of planetary motion
  - 1st law: the orbit of each planet around the sun is an ellipse with the sun at one focus
  - 2nd law: as a planet moves around its orbit, it sweeps out in equal areas in equal time.
    - The area covered by a planet in a given time is equivalent
    - The planet is orbiting faster closer to the sun and slower further Raj the sun
    - More distant planets orbit the sun at slower average speeds
