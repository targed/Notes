# Astronomical distances
- Astronomical units (AU)
  - The average distance between the sun and earth
  - 150 million KM
  - 1.5 x 10^8 KM
- Light year
  - The distance light can travel in a year
  - A measure of distance NOT time
  - around 10 trillion KM
  - Light travels at approximately 299,792,458 meters per second, or 1 light-second per second.
  - 10^13 KM
  - (Speed of light) x (1 Year) = 9,460,000,000,000 KM
  - 9.46 x 10^12 KM
