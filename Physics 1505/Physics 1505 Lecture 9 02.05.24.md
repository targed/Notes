# Particles of light
- Particles of light are called photons
- Each photon has a wavelength and a frequency
- The energy of a photon depends on its frequency
- Light is both a particle and a wave - it is wacky
- ![2024-02-05-15-12-18.jpeg](../assets/2024-02-05-15-12-18.jpeg)
- Individual photons carry very little energy but there are a lot of photons so that is why there is a lot of energy from the sun
- We can only see a small part of the electromagnetic spectrum
- The energy of light is proportional to its frequency
- Radio waves to ultraviolet waves are the only waves that can penetrate the atmosphere.
# Lights interaction with matter
- Atoms have a nucleus of protons and neutrons surrounded by a cloud of electrons
  - The cloud of electrons is a probability of the electrons
  - You can know where the electron is or its speed but not both at the same time
# Atomic terminology
- The atomic number is the number of protons in the nucleus
- Atomic mass number is the number of protons and neutrons
- Molecules are combinations of two or more atoms
- Isotopes are variations of elements but with different number of neutrons
- Carbon-14 is used for radioactive dating because we can measure the radioactive decay of carbon-14 over time
# Energy in atoms
- Elections in atoms are restricted to energy levels
- They can only change energy when it corresponds to a transition between energy levels
- Energy is ALWAYS conserved
- If an electron goes to a lower energy level it emits a photon
- If an election goes to a higher energy level it absorbs a photon
# 3 types of spectra
- Spectra of astrophysical objects are usually combinations of three basic types
  - Emission line spectra: only some special energy (frequencies) are emitted
  - Absorption line spectra: only some special energy (frequencies) are absorbed
  - Continuous spectra: all sort of absorptions and emissions
- Using a spectroscope, we can tell what elements the light has passed through and thus the composition of the medium it passed through
