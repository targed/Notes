# Inductivism vs falsification
- Scientific knowledge is processional
- Scientist try to prove themselves wrong
- A theory must be able to be tested to be conceivably false
- Science should attempt to disprove a theory
  - A theory that cannot be disproven is not a theory
- Our understanding of gravity
  - For 1500 years, people believed that heavier things fell faster
  - Then, only in India they proposed that things fall because mass attracts 
    mass
  - Then in 1600, Galileo showed that weight does not matter how fast things fall. This was inductive reasoning
  - Then Newton proposed the universal law of gravitation. He based this on data with inductive reasoning and then deduced planetary motion
  - Einstein saw that there were some discrepancies in the universal law of gravitation. He used deductive reasoning to propose general relativity

# Waves
- Waves carry energy without moving mater
- We define waves in terms of wavelength or frequency
- Wavelength
  - Is the distance between two wave peaks
  - Distance in space of a snapshot of time
- Frequency
  - Frequency is the number of times per second the wave moves up and down
  - Measure of time of a snapshot in space
- Wave speed = wavelength x frequency
- A light wave is a vibration of electric and magnetic fields; An electromagnetic wave
- Light interacts with charged particles through these electromagnetic fields
