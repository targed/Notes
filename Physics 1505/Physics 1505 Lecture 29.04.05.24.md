# The galaxy zoo
## What are the types of galaxies
- The Milky Way is one of billions in the universe
- Our deepens images of the universe show a great variety of galaxies, some of which are billions of light years awa
- A galaxies age, its distance, and the age if the universe are all closely related
- Cosmology is the study of the large scale structure of the universe
- We classify galaxies into 3 categories
  - Spiral galaxies
  - Elliptical galaxies
    - Much bigger than spiral galaxies
    - Has much more mass and many more stars
    - There shape is a sphere
    - The red color indicates that they contain old stars
  - Irregular galaxies
    - They don’t have a pre defined shape
    - They can be single galaxies or interactions between galaxies
    - The blue color indicates that there is star formation
    - This includes things like the large Magellan cloud
  - There are variations in galaxies
    - Barred galaxies
      - The disk is characterized by the arms protruding from an arm
      - The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy
      - Has a lot of star formation
    - Lenticular galaxies

## Galaxy distances
- Brightness also does not provide enough information to measure the distance to an object
- ![2024-04-05-15-33-05.jpeg](../assets/2024-04-05-15-33-05.jpeg)
- In order to measure the distance, you need ti know the brightness and the intrinsic luminosity
- A standard candle is an object whose luminosity we can determine without measuring its distance
- If you can find a standard candle in the universe, then you can use it as a baseline for measuring stuff
- We use the distance ladder
  - Start with small objects, bounce waves off of it
  - For close stars, measure the parallax
  - Step 1: determine the distance between the planets with radar
  - Step 2: determine the distance to nearby stars using the parallax
  - Step 3: There is a class of stars called the cepheids which are variable stars with a period of about 5 days and a certain luminosity. You can use these known luminosities to compute the distance
  - Step 4: white dwarf can also be used as standard candles. The apparent brightness of a white dwarf supernovae tells us the distance to its galaxy
  - Step 5: the expansion speed of the universe. The red shift the the galaxies as they get further away
