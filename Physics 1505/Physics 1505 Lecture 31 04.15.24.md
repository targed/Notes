# Hubbles law and the distance ladder
- After Hubble found variable stars in andromeda, he went looking for other variable stars in other galaxies
- He found out that the further the galaxies, the higher the velocity away from us
- ![2024-04-15-15-42-39.jpeg](../assets/2024-04-15-15-42-39.jpeg)
- We observed this phenomenon and saw that galaxies have a detectable redshift as they are going away from us faster and faster
# Hubbles law
- ![2024-04-15-15-44-36.jpeg](../assets/2024-04-15-15-44-36.jpeg)
- Hubbles law states that the velocity of the galaxies is proportional to their distance
- ![2024-04-15-15-46-29.jpeg](../assets/2024-04-15-15-46-29.jpeg)
- The relation is liberal between distance and how fast the object is going away from us
