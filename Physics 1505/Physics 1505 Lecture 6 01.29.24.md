# Galileo Galilei
- He was one of the first astronomers with a telescope
- He is one of the fathers of astronomical physics
- The copernican system had 3 key objections that Galileo overcame:
  - Earth could not be moving because objects in the air would be left behind
  - Non-circular orbits are not perfect as heavens should be
  - If earth were really orbiting a star, we’d detect stellar parallax
- Galileo showed that objects in air would stay with earth as it moves
  - Aristotle thought that all objects naturally come to a stop
  - Galileo showed that objects will stay in motion unless a force acts upon them
- To disprove the “perfection” of the universe, he looked at sunspots and created on the moon
- Galileo showed that stars a very far away so they don’t parallax
- Galileo found 4 moons around Jupiter
- Found that Venus showed phases of the moon
  - Found that Venus had an orbit inside earths
  - He looked at the sun and saw sunspots
    - Proved it was not perfect
- Galileo views were condemned by the church
