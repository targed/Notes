## Scales
- ![2024-04-19-15-20-32.jpeg](../assets/2024-04-19-15-20-32.jpeg)
- We cannot know how big the universe is because the universe is expanding
- Point where the universe is expanding faster than the speed of light is called the Hubble sphere
- However, you can see beyond 13 billion light years by seeing previous light. This is called the cosmological horizon
- If the universe is expanding but slower and slower, then light will eventually catch up to every point in the universe and you will be able to see all of the universe
- For an accelerating universe, there is a point we will NEVER be able to see past but we don’t know how big that is.
- The look back time is how young an object in the sky looks like. The younger it looks, the further it is.
- You would see the object as it was when it emitted the light
- The maximum lookback time is the edge of the universe which is 13.7 billion years
- We thought that galaxies would take at least 700 million years to form but JWST proved there were galaxies in the very early universe
- The further you go back in time, the galaxies become more irregular
