## Introduction
  
  This lecture discusses the conditions for static equilibrium, which applies to objects at rest that remain at rest.  We will explore:
  
  * Conditions for static equilibrium
  * Examples of static equilibrium problems
## Conditions for Static Equilibrium
  
  An object is in static equilibrium if it meets *both* of the following conditions:
  
  * **No linear acceleration:** $\sum \vec{F} = 0$ (The net force on the object is zero.)
  * **No angular acceleration:** $\sum \vec{\tau} = 0$ (The net torque on the object is zero.)
## Two-Dimensional Problems
  
  For two-dimensional problems (where all forces lie in the xy-plane), the conditions for static equilibrium become:
  
  * $\sum F_x = 0$
  * $\sum F_y = 0$
  * $\sum \tau_z = 0$  (Since all forces are in the xy-plane, all torques will be in the z-direction.)
## Choice of Reference Point for Torques
  
  * **Object not rotating:** Since the object is not rotating, you can choose *any* point as the reference point (pivot point) for calculating torques.  The net torque will still be zero.
  * **Reference point on line of action:** If the reference point is chosen along the line of action of a force, the moment arm for that force is zero, and thus the torque due to that force is zero. This can simplify calculations.
  * **Convenient reference points:**
    * Point where several forces act
    * Point where an unknown force acts (This eliminates the torque due to the unknown force from the equation.)
## Easy Example: Father and Son on a See-Saw
  
  **Problem:** A father (mass $m_1$) and son (mass $m_2$) are on a see-saw, which is a beam of mass $M$ and length $L$ that is pivoted in the middle. The son sits at one end. How far from the middle does the father have to sit for the see-saw to be in equilibrium?
  
  **(Solution will be discussed during the lecture. Key concepts are choosing the pivot point at the center of the see-saw and balancing the torques due to the father, son, and the weight of the see-saw itself (if the see-saw's mass is not negligible).)**
