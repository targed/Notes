## Example 1: Decimal to Binary Conversion
  
  * **Question:** Convert 215.8125 to binary.
  * **Solution:** Convert integer and fractional parts separately.  215 (integer) is converted by successive division by 2. 0.8125 (fractional) is converted by successive multiplication by 2. The result is 11010111.1101_2.
## Example 2: Unsigned Binary Addition
  
  * **Question:** Add 1100111 + 1011011.
  * **Solution:** Perform binary addition with carry-over. The result is 11000010_2.
## Example 3: Unsigned Binary Subtraction
  
  * **Question:** Subtract 11001010 - 11001.
  * **Solution:** Perform binary subtraction with borrowing. 11001010 - 00011001 = 10110001_2.
## Example 4: 2's Complement Subtraction
  
  * **Question:** Use 8-bit 2's complement to subtract 24 - 63.
  * **Solution:**  Represent 24 and 63 in 8-bit 2's complement. Find the 2's complement of 63 and add it to 24. Discard any carry beyond the 8th bit. 00011000 + 11000001 = 11110001_2 = -39_10.
