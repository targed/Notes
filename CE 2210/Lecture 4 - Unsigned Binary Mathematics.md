## Binary Addition:
  
  * Add and carry the 1s.
  * Example 1: 5 + 3 = 8
  * 5 (decimal) = 101 (binary)
  * 3 (decimal) = 011 (binary)
  * 101 + 011 = 1000 (binary) = 8 (decimal)
  * Example 2: 79 + 236 = 315
  * 79 (decimal) = 01001111 (binary)
  * 236 (decimal) = 11101100 (binary)
  * 01001111 + 11101100 = 100111011 (binary) = 315 (decimal)
## Binary Subtraction:
  
  * Subtract and borrow from a higher power.
  * Example: 84 - 29 = 55
    * 84 (decimal) = 1010100 (binary)
    * 29 (decimal) = 0011101 (binary)
    * Borrow "2" (represented as "10") from the next higher position when needed.
    * 1010100 - 0011101 = 0110111 (binary) = 55 (decimal)
## Binary Multiplication:
  
  * Multiply each of the digits in the bottom by the full top and shift over to the right.
  * Example: 6 * 5 = 30
    * 6 (decimal) = 110 (binary)
    * 5 (decimal) = 101 (binary)
    * Perform multiplication similar to decimal multiplication.
    * 110 * 101 = 11110 (binary) = 30 (decimal)
## Binary Division:
  
  * Example: 132 / 6 = 22
    * 132 (decimal) = 10000100 (binary)
    * 6 (decimal) = 110 (binary)
    * Perform long division similar to decimal division.
    * 10000100 / 110 = 10110 (binary) = 22 (decimal)
